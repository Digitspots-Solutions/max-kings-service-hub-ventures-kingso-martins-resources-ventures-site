Building Nigeria's Manufacturing Future

Max-kings Service Hub Ventures and Kingso Martins Resources Ventures represent a commitment to excellence in the Nigerian manufacturing sector. Based in the heart of Victoria Island, Lagos, we combine modern manufacturing techniques with deep understanding of local market needs.

Our facility at Bay Beach Towers serves as a hub for innovative manufacturing solutions, where we transform raw materials into quality products that meet international standards while remaining accessible to the Nigerian market.
