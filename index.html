About Us

Estherella Bureau de Change, operating as Estherella Currency Services Limited, is a fully licensed financial services institution specializing in foreign exchange transactions. Located in the heart of Victoria Island, Lagos, we bring over a decade of commercial banking expertise to every transaction. Our commitment is simple: provide transparent, efficient, and reliable currency exchange services that meet the diverse needs of Nigeria's dynamic economy. Whether you're traveling abroad, making international payments, or managing business forex requirements, Estherella delivers excellence with every exchange.
